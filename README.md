# Know_Your_Rights

Legal documents and laws are often written in complex language that ordinary citizens struggle to understand. As a result, many people sign contracts, agreements, or accept legal terms without fully knowing their rights, obligations, or potential risks. Access to legal assistance is also expensive and not easily available to everyone, especially when dealing with common issues such as rental agreements, consumer complaints, employment contracts, or online fraud.

Many individuals face legal problems like unfair contract clauses, delayed refunds, cyber fraud, or landlord disputes but lack the knowledge or resources to interpret legal documents and take appropriate action. This knowledge gap creates a barrier to justice and often leads to exploitation.

To address this issue, the project KnowRights – AI Legal Intelligence System** aims to build an AI-powered platform that helps users analyze legal documents, detect risky or unfair clauses, and explain legal information in simple, easy-to-understand language. The system leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP), Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), and Reinforcement Learning to interpret legal text and provide meaningful insights.

The platform allows users to upload legal documents, ask legal questions through a chatbot, or describe their issue using speech. The system then processes the input to identify legal domains, retrieve relevant legal information, and generate simplified explanations and recommendations. Additionally, it provides features such as legal risk detection, multilingual explanations, scam detection, complaint generation, and a legal safety score that helps users quickly understand the level of risk in a contract or situation.

By combining AI technologies with legal knowledge, the system aims to make legal information more accessible, transparent, and understandable for the general public, ultimately empowering citizens to better understand and protect their rights.
